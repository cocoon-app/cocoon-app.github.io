Good questions.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want and ask for changes in plain words. The code is there if you ever want it, behind one small button.

Can a developer take over my app later?

Yes. Cocoon writes a normal codebase on standard tools, with documentation and a full history on GitHub. Any developer can pick it up.

What does it cost to run?

You pay the AI you connect as you go; OpenRouter can start free. Publishing a website can be free. Apple charges $99 a year and Google Play $25 once, only if you ship to their stores.

Who owns what I build?

You do. The code is on your Mac and in your own GitHub, published under your own accounts. If you stop using Cocoon, everything keeps working.

How do I install it?

Download Cocoon, open the file and drag Cocoon into Applications. That's it. From then on Cocoon updates itself, and each update is checked by Apple before it installs.

What does beta mean?

Cocoon works today and is improving every week. Some things will change, and your feedback decides what comes next.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 26. Cocoon downloads everything else during a one-time setup, and shows you the progress.
