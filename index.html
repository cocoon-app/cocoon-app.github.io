FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Cocoon is made for people with ideas, not engineering degrees. You describe what you want and ask for changes in plain words. The code is there if you ever want it — tucked behind one small button.

What can I build?

Websites and web apps (shops, bookings, portfolios, dashboards, communities, games), native iPhone apps and native Android apps — including apps that need a database or an online backend.

What does it cost to run?

Cocoon connects to a cloud AI you choose, paid as you go — about $5 of credit builds many apps. Publishing a website can be free; Apple's developer program is $99/year and Google Play is a one-time $25 — only if you ship to the stores.

Who owns my apps?

You do. Your code lives on your Mac and in your own GitHub, published under your own Vercel, Fly.io, Apple and Google accounts. Stop using Cocoon tomorrow and everything you built keeps working.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 26. Cocoon downloads everything else it needs — like Apple's tools for iPhone apps — during a one-time setup, with progress you can see.
